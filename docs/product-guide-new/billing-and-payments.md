# Billing & Payments

## What this page is for
The **Billing & Payments** screen manages your platform subscription, pricing packages, payment cards, invoice lookups, and transactional history logs.

---

## Features on this page

### 1. Subscription & Usage overview
- **What it does:** Displays your current plan package (e.g. Starter vs Enterprise) and current monthly usage statistics (total calls placed, minutes consumed).
- **Why it helps you:** Tracks your current spending and warns you if you are approaching plan usage limits.
- **How to use it:** Read the subscription details card at the top of the screen.

### 2. Payment Method Card manager
- **What it does:** Secure inputs to save or update your credit card details.
- **Why it helps you:** Keeps card details updated to prevent service interruptions due to billing issues.
- **How to use it:** Click **Update Card**, enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV code, and click **Save**.

### 3. Invoices & Transaction History
- **What it does:** Lists all historically billed transactions, payment dates, transaction IDs, billing items, status (Paid, Failed), and costs.
- **Why it helps you:** Provides a record history for bookkeeping and allows download of invoices.
- **How to use it:** Scroll through the transaction table. Click on the **Download** icon next to any invoice row to export the invoice PDF.

---

## Common workflows

### Workflow A: Downloading a past invoice
1. Navigate to the **Billing** screen.
2. Scroll down to the **Transaction History** table.
3. Locate the row representing last month's subscription payment.
4. Click the **Download PDF** icon to export it for your accountant.

### Workflow B: Updating a payment method
1. Go to the **Billing** screen.
2. Locate the **Payment Method** section.
3. Click the **Edit** or **Update** button.
4. Type your new credit card details and save. The old card will be removed automatically.

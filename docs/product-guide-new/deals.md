# Deals & Sales Pipeline

## What this page is for
The **Deals** screen is a visual Kanban board representing your sales pipelines. It lets you monitor prospective deal values, drag cards across columns representing process stages, track deal statuses (In Progress, Won, Lost), and check associated call activity metrics.

---

## Features on this page

### 1. Interactive Kanban Board
- **What it does:** Displays deals as card widgets arranged in columns representing process stages (e.g. Initial Contact, Insurance Verify, Scheduled).
- **Why it helps you:** Provides an instant visual representation of your sales pipeline health. You can drag and drop cards to update their stage in real time.
- **How to use it:** Press and hold on a deal card, drag it horizontally to another column, and release it. The deal's stage is updated automatically.

### 2. Search & Pipeline Selector
- **What it does:** Allows you to search deals by name or client and choose between different active pipelines (e.g. Patient Intake vs. Payment Reminders) using a dropdown selector.
- **Why it helps you:** Keeps separate sales flows organized so that agents only see relevant deals for their active campaign.
- **How to use it:** Use the search field at the top left of the board, or click the dropdown menu next to it to switch active pipelines.

### 3. Add Deal Popup Form
- **What it does:** Opens a creation form where you specify the Deal Name, associated Client, Deal Amount, Currency, Owner, and initial Pipeline Stage.
- **Why it helps you:** Allows you to log and track financial values associated with client milestones manually.
- **How to use it:** Click the blue **+ Add Deal** button at the top right, fill in the field details, and click **Save**.

### 4. Deal Status Badges (Won/Lost)
- **What it does:** Allows marking a deal as "Won" or "Lost" via inline card actions, updating totals and pipeline statistics.
- **Why it helps you:** Helps clean up your board while keeping record history for final conversion analytics.
- **How to use it:** Click the three dots on a deal card and select **Mark Won** or **Mark Lost**. The card will be archived off the active board.

## Common workflows

### Workflow A: Advancing a deal following manual verification
1. Find the deal card "Patient Intake Package" for "Sarah Johnson".
2. After verification is complete, click and drag the card from the "Initial Contact" column to the "Insurance Verify" column.
3. The system will record the stage change, updating the pipeline value statistics.

### Workflow B: Closing a won deal
1. Click the menu button (three dots) on the top right of the "SARAH JOHNSON - Intake Package" card.
2. Select **Mark Won** from the options.
3. The board total value will adjust and the win will be tracked in organization statistics.

# Team & User Management

## What this page is for
The **Users & Team** screens allow you to invite team members, set up user access roles, configure calendar sync settings, edit staff shifts, and manage vacation dates.

---

## Features on this page

### 1. Team Directory List
- **What it does:** Displays all team members registered under the active branch organization, detailing their email address, role, and active status switch.
- **Why it helps you:** Keeps a centralized list of staff, tracking who can access platform tools.
- **How to use it:** Scroll the staff rows. Toggle the status switch to suspend or activate a user account.

### 2. Invite Team Member Form
- **What it does:** A popup screen to invite team members by email, assign their role, and set permissions.
- **Why it helps you:** Allows you to control who can view or modify settings (e.g. allowing call viewing but blocking billing updates).
- **How to use it:** Click **+ Add User**, fill in name/email, check Core, Operations, or System module checkboxes, and click **Send Invitation**.

### 3. Personal Info & Shift Availability
- **What it does:** Configures the staff member's timezone, language, and weekly hours (start and end times per weekday).
- **Why it helps you:** Restricts the AI receptionist from scheduling client appointments outside of official working hours.
- **How to use it:** Under the user's profile tab, select **Availability**, check the working days, and adjust the start/end times.

### 4. Calendar Sync Connections
- **What it does:** Connects Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar to the user's staff account.
- **Why it helps you:** Allows the AI receptionist to sync calendar slots, preventing booking conflicts.
- **How to use it:** Go to the member's profile calendar settings and click **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Outlook**.

### 5. Vacation & Days Off Planner
- **What it does:** Blocks out specific dates in the team member's schedule (e.g. holidays or sick leave).
- **Why it helps you:** Prevents automated call flows from booking appointments when staff are away.
- **How to use it:** Select **Days Off**, pick dates in the calendar grid, and click **Add**.

## Common workflows

### Workflow A: Inviting a new receptionist agent
1. Click **+ Add User** in the team dashboard.
2. Input "Sarah Jenkins" and email. Set Role to **Agent**.
3. Under permissions check: Core modules -> **Write**, Operations -> **View**, and System -> **None**.
4. Click **Invite**. Sarah receives an invitation email.

### Workflow B: Syncing calendars and setting shifts
1. Under **Team**, open "Sarah Jenkins" profile.
2. Click **Availability** tab, check Monday through Friday, and input shift times: **09:00 AM - 05:00 PM**.
3. Click the **Calendar** tab and authenticate with her work Google Calendar account.
4. Click **Save**. The AI is now ready to book appointments directly onto her synchronized schedule.

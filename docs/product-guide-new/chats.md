# Live Chat Messaging

## What this page is for
The **Chats** screen is your hub for live text communications. It aggregates incoming SMS, WhatsApp, and Web Chat messages into a single inbox, allowing team members to monitor conversations, take over manually from the AI receptionist, and text clients directly.

---

## Features on this page

### 1. Inbox Conversations Sidebar
- **What it does:** Lists all active chat threads, displaying the client's name, a snippet of the latest message, time sent, and unread badges. It also highlights whether the AI is currently auto-replying or if the thread is assigned to a human.
- **Why it helps you:** Keeps all text communications organized. You can filter threads to only show "Unread" or "Assigned to Me" chats.
- **How to use it:** Scroll the inbox list. Click on any contact name to load their messaging history.

### 2. Message History Canvas
- **What it does:** Displays the historic message log in chronological order, with chat bubbles separating the Client's texts from the AI/Human's responses.
- **Why it helps you:** Allows you to read the full context of what the client has asked and what details have been shared before you reply.
- **How to use it:** Scroll up or down within the chat window to read past messages.

### 3. Messaging Editor Bar
- **What it does:** A text entry panel at the bottom of the chat window with quick template drop-downs, emoji selector buttons, file attachments, and a send button.
- **Why it helps you:** Allows manual agents to compose, format, and send responses. Quick templates save time on common responses (e.g. business hours or directions).
- **How to use it:** Click the text input box, type your message, and click the blue **Send** arrow or press Enter. Click the paperclip icon to upload documents or images.

### 4. Human Takeover Toggle Switch
- **What it does:** A switch at the top of the chat panel marked "AI Receptionist Auto-Reply". When toggled off, the AI is paused, and the chat is assigned to "Human Representative".
- **Why it helps you:** Essential when a client asks a complex or sensitive question that the AI receptionist cannot handle. Pausing the AI prevents it from interrupting while a human staff member chats.
- **How to use it:** Toggle the **AI Auto-Reply** switch in the chat header. The system will alert the client and hand over manual keyboard control to you.

### 5. Client Metadata Panel (Right Sidebar)
- **What it does:** A collapsible summary panel showing the client's email, phone, location, current workflow process stage, and an interactive notes box.
- **Why it helps you:** Provides client details on the spot so you don't have to navigate to the Clients tab while texting them.
- **How to use it:** Read the details panel on the right side of the chat layout. Click inside the **Notes** block to type reminders and save them to their customer profile.

## Common workflows

### Workflow A: Intervening when a client gets confused
1. Monitor the chat inbox. Look for a conversation showing a "Frustrated Sentiment" alert or notification.
2. Click the client's name in the inbox sidebar to load the conversation.
3. Read the message history to see where the AI struggled to answer.
4. Toggle the **AI Receptionist Auto-Reply** switch to **Off** in the chat header.
5. Type your response in the editor bar (e.g. "Hi, I'm taking over this chat to help you directly...") and press Send.

### Workflow B: Sending a quick booking template link
1. Open the chat thread for "Sarah Johnson".
2. Toggle AI Auto-Reply to **Off**.
3. Click the **Templates** button in the reply bar.
4. Select the "Booking Link" pre-saved template.
5. Click **Send**. The template text and link are sent to the client, and the action is saved in their profile timeline.

# Overview Dashboard

## What this page is for
The **Overview Dashboard** is the dashboard home screen in MantraAssist. It provides a visual, real-time summary of call volumes, successfully completed calls, average call length, call costs, and client conversion rates across stages.

---

## Features on this page

### 1. High-Level Performance Metrics
- **What it does:** Displays four stats cards: Total Call Volume, Completed Calls, Success Rate, and Average Call Duration.
- **Why it helps you:** Gives you an instant understanding of your operations. Positive or negative indicators show how current performance compares to the previous period.
- **How to use it:** View the large numbers at the top of the dashboard.

### 2. Call Volume & Trend Charts
- **What it does:** Displays a line and area chart mapping call volumes day-by-day (Monday through Sunday) alongside a comparison of completed and failed calls.
- **Why it helps you:** Helps you identify peak days and hours of the week so you can adjust schedule rules or outbound calling plans.
- **How to use it:** Hover your mouse over any point on the chart lines to see a pop-up breakdown of call counts for that specific day.

### 3. Conversion Funnel Chart
- **What it does:** Displays a step-down bar chart representing the percentage of clients who successfully transition from one stage to another (e.g. from Initial Contact to Insurance Verification to Appointment).
- **Why it helps you:** Pinpoints exactly where clients drop off in your workflow so you can fix bottlenecks (such as slow insurance verification processes).
- **How to use it:** Read the percentage markers on each stage bar. A sharp drop highlights an area that needs operational review.

### 4. Interactive Filters Bar
- **What it does:** Allows you to filter all statistics on the page by date range (1 week, 1 month, 3 months, or a custom range), specific Process, specific Stage, Call Type (Inbound vs. Outbound), and Industry.
- **Why it helps you:** Lets you zero in on a single process (like "New Patient Intake") or time period to see how that specific area of your business is performing, rather than looking at cluttered mixed data.
- **How to use it:** 
  1. Click the **Filters** button at the top right of the dashboard.
  2. Select your desired Process, Stage, Call Type, or Industry from the drop-down menus.
  3. Click **1W**, **1M**, or **3M** to quickly switch periods, or input specific start and end dates under the custom date range section.
  4. The graphs and metric cards will update automatically.

### 5. "How MantraAssist Works" Guide Button
- **What it does:** Opens a step-by-step interactive popup guide explaining the core concepts of the platform.
- **Why it helps you:** Serves as a quick refresher if you or a team member forget how call routing, stages, or automation work.
- **How to use it:** Click the **How MantraAssist Works** button next to the filters button at the top right of the page. Use the "Next" and "Back" buttons in the popup to browse the guide.

## Common workflows

### Workflow A: Checking new process drop-offs
1. Click the **Filters** button.
2. Select your new campaign from the **Process** drop-down.
3. Review the **Conversion Funnel** chart. Find where the bars shrink significantly. If you see a major drop-off at the "Insurance Verify" stage, alert your billing team to check if verification requests are getting stuck.

### Workflow B: Reviewing weekend call performance
1. Click the **Filters** button and select **Custom Date Range**.
2. Pick Saturday and Sunday dates for the past weekend.
3. Look at the **Call Volume & Trend** chart. Review the success rate.
4. Verify if call failures were higher than weekdays, which could indicate a need to adjust voicemail detection rules or adjust call-back retry schedules for weekends.

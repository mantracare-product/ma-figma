# Services Management

## What this page is for
The **Services** page allows you to define your business offerings (e.g. "General Checkup," "Insurance Evaluation," or "Consultation"). The AI receptionist uses these services to quote pricing, check staff availability, and schedule appointments on your calendar.

---

## Features on this page

### 1. Services directory List
- **What it does:** Displays a table of all your services with columns showing the Service Name, Cost, Duration (in minutes), Description, and Active Status.
- **Why it helps you:** Keeps your service catalogs organized in one place, allowing fast updates to pricing or descriptions.
- **How to use it:** View the service cards. Click on the action menu next to any service row to edit parameters or delete the service.

### 2. Add Service Popup Form
- **What it does:** Opens a creation form requiring Service Name, Price, Duration (minutes), Description, and Assignees.
- **Why it helps you:** Allows you to offer new products or consultation packages that clients can book through the AI receptionist.
- **How to use it:** Click the blue **+ Add Service** button at the top right, fill in the parameters, and click **Save**.

### 3. Service Status toggles
- **What it does:** A switch to toggle services between "Active" and "Inactive".
- **Why it helps you:** Temporarily disables bookings for seasonal offerings or fully booked service lines without deleting their templates.
- **How to use it:** Toggle the switch in the status column of the service row.

---

## Common workflows

### Workflow A: Adding a "Teeth Cleaning" dental service
1. Go to the **Services** screen and click **+ Add Service**.
2. Type Name "Teeth Cleaning", Cost "$120", and Duration "45" minutes.
3. Add a description: "Standard teeth cleaning and tartar check."
4. Check the box to assign Dr. Robert Martinez to this service.
5. Click **Save**. The AI receptionist will now offer this service to callers and book it onto Dr. Martinez's schedule.

### Workflow B: Adjusting service pricing
1. Find "General Consultation" in the services table.
2. Click the edit pencil icon on its row.
3. Update the cost field from "$75" to "$90".
4. Click **Save**. The AI receptionist will instantly start quoting the updated price to callers.
